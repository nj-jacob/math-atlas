# Changelog

Newest first. One line per change, in plain English.

## 2026-10-31
- Grade 4: spinners, bar graphs, growing patterns, the robot path and the shape slide show real orange and green again, so the words in the questions match the pictures (the shared theme had turned them teal).
- Coin "build it" questions now work in every grade, and one shared helper (`Atlas.Q.shapes`) wires question shapes into a chapter, so grades don't copy it.
- Match cards and "why?" reasons fit on a phone screen in every grade (long words and sentences wrap instead of running off the side).
- Checks: fresh quizzes are now answered the same way as practice, so fraction answers (like 1/2) are typed correctly.
- Grade 8: every practice skill in all 11 chapters now asks in 4 to 6 different ways (stories, number lines, true or false with a reason, matching, ordering, fill the blanks, tap, pick all, and "which way?" choices). Each chapter quiz is fresh every time.
- Grade 8 "Big and Small Numbers": match powers of ten, order them, spot proper scientific notation, metric prefix stairs.
- Grade 8 "Squares, Roots and Real Numbers": place roots on a number line, tap the perfect square below, sort rational from irrational, order real numbers.
- Grade 8 "Integers": number-line sums and differences, temperature and diving stories, sign mistakes, "which way?" for word problems.
- Grade 8 "Fractions": rename-then-add steps, the add-the-bottoms mistake, recipe and ribbon stories, "which way?" for fraction word problems.
- Grade 8 "Percents, Ratios and Rates": percent on a number line, sale-price "which way?", HST stories, equal-ratio picks, best-buy taps.
- Grade 8 "Patterns, Expressions and Equations": write the rule, match rate and initial value, solve step by step, inequality picks and number lines.
- Grade 8 "Data and Graphs": sort continuous data, match data to graphs, read scatter plots, misleading-graph checks, trace code step by step.
- Grade 8 "Probability": probability line, raffle and coin-and-die stories, independent or dependent, Venn diagram parts.
- Grade 8 "Angles, Shapes and Moves": missing angles on a line, parallel-line angle picks, polygon angle sums, transformation rules.
- Grade 8 "Pythagoras, Area and Volume": Pythagoras step by step, hypotenuse on a number line, circle, composite area, volume and surface area stories.
- Grade 8 "Money and Planning": exchange "which way?", budget surplus on a line, HST taps, interest stories, coupon and phone-plan choices.
- Every grade now uses one shared copy of the lesson engine and toolbox (`assets/engine/`) instead of eight copies.
- New answer types for every grade: tap the picture, pick all that apply, put in order, number line, match pairs, true or false and why, fill the blanks, build with blocks or coins.
- Grade 3 "Numbers to 1000": every practice skill asks in 4 to 7 different ways. Fresh chapter quiz every time.
- Grade 4 "Numbers to 10 000": every practice skill asks in 5 ways (tap a digit, build with blocks, number line, match, mistakes to spot). Fresh chapter quiz every time.
- Grade 4 "Add and Subtract": every skill asks in 4 or 5 ways, including "which way?" (add or subtract, and the quickest mental method), stories and mistakes to spot. Fresh chapter quiz.
- Grade 4 "Multiply and Divide": every skill asks in 4 or 5 ways, including tap the array, area-model blanks, "which way?" (multiply or divide, and what to do with leftovers) and a rate number line. Fresh chapter quiz.
- Grade 4 "Fractions": every skill asks in 4 or 5 ways, including tap and match fraction bars, place fractions on a number line, and put fractions in order. Fresh chapter quiz.
- Grade 4 "Decimals": every skill asks in 4 or 5 ways, including tenths on a number line, tap the point, rounding on the line and "which way?". Fresh chapter quiz.
- Grade 4 "Patterns and Equations": every skill asks in 4 or 5 ways, including tap the next shape, fill the table, "which way?" to solve, and the greatest answer on a number line. Fresh chapter quiz.
- Grade 4 "Data": every skill asks in 4 ways, including read a stem-and-leaf plot onto a number line, order spinners by likelihood, and match mode, median and range. Fresh chapter quiz.
- Grade 4 "Shapes, Angles and Grids": every skill asks in 4 or 5 ways, including tap angles, tap points on the grid for slides and flips, and pick the rectangles. Fresh chapter quiz.
- Grade 4 "Measuring": every skill asks in 4 or 5 ways, including masses and jugs on a number line, an elapsed-time line, and tap the rectangle with a given area. Fresh chapter quiz.
- Grade 4 "Money": every skill asks in 4 ways, including count up to the change on a number line, a savings table, and tap the best price. Fresh chapter quiz.
