Decodable readers

Phonics stories for Kindergarten to Grade 3, following Ontario's Language Foundations Continuum and taught the Orton-Gillingham way. Each story uses only the sounds taught so far, with a word hunt and a grown-up page. In the Toolbox, on the Reading shelf.

  • Live

Spelling

Spelling taught step by step in the same order as the decodable readers, the Orton-Gillingham way: sound boxes, sorts, endings, a dictation the grown-up marks sound by sound, and a page to print. In the Toolbox, on the Spelling shelf.
