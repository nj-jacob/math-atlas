Find the right starting point

A short placement check finds where your child is in each part of math, from Grade 1 to Grade 8. Then it builds a plan that works on a few ideas each week.

  1. Placement checkAbout 30 questions, about 25 minutes. It gets easier or harder as you go.
  2. Weekly planFour ideas at a time, gaps first, each linked to its lesson.
  3. Check-insTen questions each week. The plan moves on, or steps back.
